Privacy Policy - Odesli Link Generator

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Information We Collect

This Chrome extension does not collect personal information and has no server of its own. Nothing is sent to the developer. The extension only acts when you click its icon, and then it:

  • Reads the URL of the current tab
  • For song.link: opens song.link with this music URL to look up the universal song.link page
  • For Apple Music: reads the song's title and artist from Spotify's public embed page or from the song.link page, then searches Apple's public iTunes Search API with title and artist
  • For Spotify: looks up the Apple Music track ID in Apple's public iTunes Search API, or reads title and artist from the song.link page, and builds a Spotify search link
  • Copies the result to your clipboard and shows a short confirmation on the current page

The only thing the extension stores is your choice in the settings (song.link, Apple Music or Spotify), saved with Chrome's storage so it applies on all your devices.

Data Usage

The Odesli Link Generator extension:

  • Does not collect or store personal data
  • Does not track user behavior or analytics
  • Does not use cookies
  • Only processes the music link of the tab where you clicked the icon
  • Does not share data with third parties, except the music link, track title and artist sent to the services below to perform the conversion you requested

Third-Party Services

  • song.link by Odesli: The music URL is sent to song.link, which returns the matching song.link page with the song's title and artist.
  • Apple iTunes Search API (itunes.apple.com): Track title and artist, or the Apple Music track ID, are sent to Apple's public search to find the matching track. The store country is taken from your browser language.
  • Spotify (open.spotify.com): The public embed page of a Spotify track is loaded to read its title and artist.

Please refer to the privacy policies of Odesli, Apple and Spotify for information about their data handling practices.

Permissions Used

  • activeTab: To read the URL of the current music page when you click the icon
  • scripting: To copy the link and show a short confirmation on the current page
  • clipboardWrite: To copy the link to the clipboard
  • storage: To save which link the icon copies
  • host_permissions: To reach song.link, album.link and artist.link, Spotify's public embed pages on open.spotify.com and Apple's iTunes Search API on itunes.apple.com

Data Security

All processing happens in your browser. Requests go directly over HTTPS to song.link, open.spotify.com and itunes.apple.com. No sensitive information is stored or transmitted.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the contact information on the Chrome Web Store listing.
